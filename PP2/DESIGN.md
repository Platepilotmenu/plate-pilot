## Brand & Style

This design system is calibrated for hyper-legible, modern retail and service environments demanding rapid visual processing. The aesthetic is stripped of all rustic espresso undertones, browns, and multi-colored status noise. Instead, it adopts a razor-sharp, high-energy two-color philosophy: pure, luminous white paired exclusively with vibrant energetic orange.

The design movement is **High-Contrast Modernist Minimalism**:
- **Pure Two-Color Discipline:** Uncompromising focus using pure white backgrounds and energetic orange accents. The interface feels modern, hyper-clean, optimistic, and fast.
- **Sunlight & Fast-Paced Legibility:** High luminance contrast that eliminates glare issues on mobile and tablet terminals, with clean charcoal/black typography cutting sharply through light surfaces.
- **Electric Accent Rhythm:** Orange acts as the singular beacon for user attention—driving primary actions, focus rings, status indicators, badges, and active navigation states without competing hues.

## Colors

The palette is strictly restricted to pure crisp whites, subtle architectural light neutrals, and electric orange. All coffee browns, espresso tones, amber gradients, blues, and multi-color alert hues (greens, purples, reds) have been eradicated.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#F97316` / Vibrant Orange):** The system's primary workhorse. Applied to primary call-to-action buttons, key data highlights, active tab indicators, selection borders, and interactive controls.
- **Deep Accent (`#EA580C` / Deep Orange):** Hover and pressed states for orange interactive elements, as well as high-contrast active text on light backgrounds.
- **Subtle Orange Surface (`#FFF7ED` / Soft Tint):** Used for selected row backgrounds, chip fills, and subtle focus states, maintaining brand warmth without introducing a third hue.
- **Canvas & Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#FAFAFA`):** Crisp pure white serves as the default canvas, while `#FAFAFA` and `#F4F4F5` provide structural separation for secondary containers, table headers, and backdrop rails.
- **Text & Contrast (`#09090B` & `#71717A`):** Deep charcoal-black for headlines, SKU numbers, and currency values; muted zinc for secondary descriptors and timestamps.
- **Status & Alerts:** All status variations (ready, in-progress, urgent, completed) are handled strictly through orange accents, filled/outlined badges, typographic weight, and high-contrast iconography rather than disparate colors.

## Typography

The type scale pairs the structured, geometric energy of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral clarity of **Work Sans**.

- **Space Grotesk** commands headlines, numeric sums, tags, and micro-labels. All numerical calculations, inventory counts, and price tags require `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter during active recalculation.
- **Work Sans** handles order item descriptions, modifier lists, and body copy.
- Hierarchy is established through stark size and weight steps rather than multi-color styling. Deep orange (`#EA580C`) is used selectively for callout figures and active titles, while `#09090B` anchors default content.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a tight, responsive spatial rhythm designed for rapid touch accuracy and quick visual parsing.

- **Handheld Mobile (< 600px):** Single-column stacked stream. CTAs anchor to the screen base directly above the fixed bottom navigation with minimum 52px touch heights.
- **Tablet & POS Split (600px - 1024px):** 60/40 horizontal split. The left 60% contains a dense multi-column product selection grid; the right 40% locks the ticket sheet with sticky orange checkout actions.
- **Desktop / Wide Display (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with 1.25rem gutters. Cards align cleanly along light grid lines.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing between atomic elements follows strict 4px/8px multiples (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to preserve clean white space without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through subtle tonal contrasts and orange-accented focus borders, deliberately avoiding heavy brown or muddy shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` or pale `#FAFAFA` base.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px solid border in `#E4E4E7`. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Highlighted Tile):** Solid `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1.5px `#F97316` boundary and light tinted orange glow: `0 4px 12px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Sheets, Drawers & Popovers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px `#E4E4E7` border with a clean neutral shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Pressed State:** Instant 1px downward translation with a high-contrast `#EA580C` border to deliver snappy tactile validation.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px base) to balance structural precision with welcoming tactility.

- **Standard Containers, Cards & Inputs:** Formed with `0.5rem (8px)` corners for crisp alignment.
- **Buttons & Action Bars:** Cut with `rounded-lg (1rem / 16px)` for approachable, thumb-friendly hit targets.
- **Counters, Badges & Pills:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px`) for order status flags, quantity markers, and active tab highlights.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Checkout, Charge, Confirm):** Minimum 52px height. Background `#F97316`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk SemiBold 16px`. Hover: `#EA580C`. Active: slight inset scale (0.98).
- **Secondary Action:** Minimum 48px height. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #F97316`, text `#EA580C`.
- **Ghost / Neutral Buttons:** Background `#F4F4F5`, text `#09090B`, no border. Hover: `#E4E4E7`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E4E4E7` border, 48px height, 16px text size to prevent mobile auto-zoom.
- Focused state: 2px solid `#F97316` outline with zero exterior blur.

### Cards & Order Tickets
- Background `#FFFFFF` framed with 1px `#E4E4E7`.
- Selected/Active card: 2px solid `#F97316` border with background `#FFF7ED`.
- Ticket status banner: Clean 3px top border using `#F97316` for active/urgent tickets and `#E4E4E7` for settled tickets.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- **Active / Primary Badge:** Background `#F97316`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Pending / In-Progress Badge:** Background `#FFF7ED`, border `1px solid #FED7AA`, text `#EA580C`.
- **Neutral / Completed Badge:** Background `#F4F4F5`, border `1px solid #E4E4E7`, text `#71717A`.

### Bottom Navigation
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border-top `1px solid #E4E4E7`, 64px height.
- Inactive tabs: `#71717A` icons and text.
- Active tab: `#F97316` icon and label, accompanied by an electric orange micro-dot or pill-shaped underline.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Steppers
- Radio and checkboxes: `#FFFFFF` surface with 1.5px border `#E4E4E7`. Selected state: fill `#F97316` with crisp white inner mark.
- Quantity steppers: `#FFFFFF` pill container with `#E4E4E7` border. Step buttons use deep charcoal `#09090B` or vibrant orange `#F97316` on touch.
